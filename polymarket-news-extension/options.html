✈ Telegram

To receive Telegram channel messages you need a Telegram Bot.

  1. Message @BotFather on Telegram and create a new bot with /newbot.
  2. Copy your bot token (format: 123456:ABC-DEF…) and paste it below.
  3. Add the bot as a member (or admin) to the channels you want to monitor.
  4. List those channel usernames below (without @).

The bot must be a member of each channel listed here.
